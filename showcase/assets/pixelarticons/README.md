# Pixelarticons assets

These local SVGs use Pixelarticons' base (non-sharp) style and remain covered by the adjacent MIT license.

- `play.svg`, `download.svg`, `info-box.svg`, `close.svg`, and `delete.svg` come from the free `halfmage/pixelarticons` repository.
- `pause.svg` comes from the MIT-licensed `pixelarticons@1.8.1` package, before that asset moved out of the current free package.
- `crown.svg` comes from the free [Pixelarticons source at `8275e0af`](https://github.com/halfmage/pixelarticons/blob/8275e0af7c16aa40c54ea2b90b7af83b1fe4eb4c/svg/crown.svg) and is used by the Clash Royale statistics and battle results.
- `sword.svg` comes from the free [Pixelarticons source at `8275e0af`](https://github.com/halfmage/pixelarticons/blob/8275e0af7c16aa40c54ea2b90b7af83b1fe4eb4c/svg/sword.svg) and is used for the Clash Royale battle counter.

- `potion.svg` comes from the free [Pixelarticons source at `8275e0af`](https://github.com/halfmage/pixelarticons/blob/8275e0af7c16aa40c54ea2b90b7af83b1fe4eb4c/svg/potion.svg) and represents Clash Royale elixir costs.

The files are vendored so these app icons have no external runtime dependency.
